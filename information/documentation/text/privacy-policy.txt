PRIVACY POLICY

NexusByte Initiative

Effective Date: 08 August 2026, 01:30 UTC


1. INTRODUCTION
This Privacy Policy explains how NexusByte Initiative ("NexusByte Initiative", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information
in connection with any web page, website, web application, WebView application, web game, browser extension, or other digital product, project, or service
that we operate (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy applies to all Users of the Services, regardless of the platform or device used to access them. By using the Services, you acknowledge
that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where required by applicable law, we will ask for your consent before collecting certain categories of
information, as described in Section 5.


2. SCOPE
This Privacy Policy covers information collected directly by NexusByte Initiative through the Services. It does not cover the practices of third-party
websites, applications, or services that may be linked to or integrated with the Services, or third-party translation or analytics providers acting on their
own behalf. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you interact with.


3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We only collect the categories of information described in this section, and only where you have provided consent as described in Section 5, or where
collection is limited to information strictly necessary to operate the Services (such as your saved theme and language preferences).

3.1 Network Information
Where consented to, we may collect information about the network and connection through which you access the Services, including but not limited to: your
approximate network connection type and effective connection speed (for example, the type of connection reported by your browser, such as Wi-Fi or cellular,
and estimated bandwidth and round-trip latency); whether your device is currently online; the web address, hostname, and protocol of the page you are viewing;
the referring web address that directed you to the Services, where available; your device's detected time zone and time zone offset; and your Internet
Protocol (IP) address and associated general geographic location, which may be derived by us or by third-party service providers acting on our behalf.

3.2 Browser Information
Where consented to, we may collect information about the browser you use to access the Services, including but not limited to: your browser's user agent
string, name, version, vendor, and rendering engine details; your browser's higher-precision platform, architecture, and version information where made
available by your browser; your preferred language and full list of configured languages; whether cookies are enabled; whether a "Do Not Track" or similar
signal has been sent; the number of installed browser plugins and supported file types, where applicable; your browser window and viewport dimensions; the
character encoding and title of the page being viewed; and whether local storage is available and functioning on your device.

3.3 Device Information
Where consented to, we may collect information about the device you use to access the Services, including but not limited to: your operating system platform;
the number of logical processors available to your browser; the approximate amount of device memory available, where made available by your browser; whether
your device supports touch input, and the maximum number of simultaneous touch points supported; your screen and available screen dimensions, colour depth,
pixel depth, device pixel ratio, and screen orientation; your device's current battery charge level, charging status, and estimated charge or discharge time,
where made available by your browser; and your device's preferred colour scheme, reduced motion, and contrast accessibility settings.

3.4 Preference Information
Regardless of your consent choice, we store your selected theme and selected language locally on your device using browser local storage, so that your
preferences are remembered and applied consistently across your visits to the Services. This preference information is not transmitted to us or to any third
party and remains on your device unless you clear your browser storage.

3.5 Consent Records
We locally record whether you have made a cookie and data collection consent choice, and if so, whether you accepted or declined, along with the date and
time of that choice, so that we do not repeatedly prompt you for consent.


4. HOW INFORMATION IS COLLECTED
Information described in Section 3 is collected using cookies, browser local storage, and standard web browser and device application programming interfaces
made available to websites and applications by your browser or operating system. We do not use invasive fingerprinting techniques, and we do not access your
camera, microphone, precise real-time location, contacts, files, or other sensitive device functionality without separately requesting your explicit
permission through your browser or device at the time such functionality is used, where applicable.


5. YOUR CONSENT CHOICE
The first time you visit or use a Service, you will be presented with a consent popup asking whether you accept or decline the collection of Network
Information, Browser Information, and Device Information as described in Sections 3.1 through 3.3.

	(a) If you select "Accept", we will collect and process the categories of information described in Sections 3.1, 3.2, and 3.3, and may share that
	information with the third-party service providers described in Section 7.
	(b) If you select "Decline", we will not collect or process the information described in Sections 3.1, 3.2, or 3.3. We will only store your consent
	choice itself, and your theme and language preferences as described in Sections 3.4 and 3.5, locally on your device.
	(c) If you close the popup without selecting either option, no consent choice will be recorded, no information described in Sections 3.1 through 3.3 will
	be collected, and you will be presented with the same popup again the next time you visit or use a Service.

You may change your consent choice at any time through the cookie and data preference controls made available within the Services, where provided, or by
clearing your browser's local storage for the relevant Service, which will cause the consent popup to be presented again on your next visit.


6. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
Where you have consented to the collection of Network, Browser, and Device Information, we use that information for the following purposes: to help the
Services load and render more quickly and reliably on your specific device, browser, and network conditions on future visits; to diagnose, troubleshoot,
and fix technical errors and compatibility issues; to understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the design, performance, accessibility, and
functionality of the Services; to detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and to comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.


7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICE PROVIDERS
Where you have consented to data collection, we may share the information described in Sections 3.1 through 3.3 with third-party service providers who
process it on our behalf for the purposes described in Section 6, including providers in the following categories: web and product analytics platforms;
error monitoring and diagnostic platforms; session recording and product experience platforms; tag and consent management platforms; and secure data storage
and processing infrastructure providers. Each third-party service provider processes information subject to its own privacy policy and data processing terms,
and we take reasonable steps to work only with providers who maintain appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

Where a Service offers on-demand text translation as an alternative to a locally stored language file, and you choose to use that feature, the text you
select for translation may be transmitted to a third-party translation service provider for processing. This occurs only when you actively use the live
translation feature, and is separate from the theme and language preference storage described in Section 3.4.


8. DATA RETENTION
Information stored locally on your device, such as your theme preference, language preference, and consent record, remains on your device until you clear
your browser's storage or uninstall the relevant Service. Information collected and transmitted to us or to our third-party service providers under Section 7
is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in Section 6, or as required by applicable law, after which it is deleted or
anonymised.


9. DATA SECURITY
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.


10. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES
Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights in relation to information we hold about you, including the right to request access to, correction of,
deletion of, or a copy of your information, the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and the right to withdraw consent at any time, which will
not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before your withdrawal. You may exercise these rights by contacting us using the details in Section 15,
or, where applicable, through cookie and privacy preference controls made available within the Services. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a
data protection supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.


11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
The Services are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or such higher minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect
information from children below that age. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child without appropriate parental or guardian consent,
we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.


12. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
Information we collect may be stored, processed, and transferred to countries other than your own, including countries that may have different data protection
laws than your jurisdiction. Where we transfer information internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure that it is protected consistently with this
Privacy Policy and applicable law.


13. DO NOT TRACK SIGNALS
Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. We currently record whether this signal has been sent as part of Browser Information under Section 3.2, where
consent has been given, but we do not automatically alter our data collection practices solely in response to that signal at this time, given the lack of a
common industry standard for its interpretation.


14. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Where changes are material, we will provide reasonable notice, for example by updating the effective date
at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.


15. CONTACT US
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any of your rights described above, please contact us through the official contact
channels published on our website.
