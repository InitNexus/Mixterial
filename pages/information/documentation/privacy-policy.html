Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data NexusByte Initiative ("NBI", "we") collects, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

Effective: 10 October 2026, 13:20 UTC · Version 1.0
Data controller: NexusByte Initiative · init.nexusbyte@gmail.com

1. Who We Are

NexusByte Initiative ("NexusByte", "NBI") is the data controller for the personal data described in this Policy. NBI operates remotely; all members work from home and we maintain no fixed physical address. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO); all privacy matters are handled through our single contact point.

Privacy contact: init.nexusbyte@gmail.com

2. Scope

This Policy applies to personal data we process when you:

  • Visit or interact with our website;
  • Purchase any product within our Product categories — Consumables, Apparel & Accessories, or Hardware;
  • Engage any service within our Service categories — Software & Subscriptions, Digital Content, Technical Services, Platform Services, or Enquiry Services;
  • Contact us by email, Discord, or any other channel.

3. What We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity data — name, username, date of birth (where age verification is required), and government-issued identification (only where legally required, or where we need to verify ownership, age, or authority).
  • Contact data — email address, phone number, delivery address, and platform usernames where relevant.
  • Transactional data — orders, invoices, payments, refunds, and correspondence.
  • Payment data — processed by third-party payment processors; we do not store full card numbers or crypto private keys.
  • Service data — information you provide when engaging our services, including proof of account ownership for Account Recovery, enquiry briefs and any third-party information you choose to provide for Enquiry Services, and device details for Technical Services.
  • Credential data — where you provide platform credentials for the limited purpose of Account Recovery through official channels, we hold them only for the duration of the engagement and destroy them afterwards.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser, device, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and timestamps.
  • Usage data — how you use our site and services.
  • Marketing preferences — your consent status for marketing communications.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account;
  • To fulfil orders and deliver products;
  • To provide and administer services across our Service categories;
  • To process payments and issue refunds;
  • To verify age and eligibility for Age-Restricted Goods;
  • To verify ownership and authorisation before performing Account Recovery or Technical Services;
  • To communicate with you about orders, services, and support;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • To comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations;
  • To improve our site, services, and products;
  • To send marketing communications (with your consent, or as otherwise permitted by law).

7. Who We Share Data With

We share personal data only where necessary, and only with parties who are contractually bound to protect it:

  • Payment processors — third-party providers, to process payments.
  • Shipping and courier partners — to deliver physical goods.
  • Cloud, hosting, and IT providers — to operate our site and services.
  • Analytics and marketing providers — where you have consented.
  • Professional advisers — legal, accounting, and insurance, under confidentiality.
  • Legal and regulatory authorities — where required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data.

8. International Transfers

Because we operate internationally, your data may be transferred outside the UK or EEA. Where it is, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA), EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), or an adequacy decision.

10. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or alteration. These include encryption in transit, access controls, least-privilege principles, and confidentiality obligations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion where we no longer have a lawful basis.
  • Restriction — limit how we use your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object — to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is consent-based.
  • Complain — to your local supervisory authority. In the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, contact init.nexusbyte@gmail.com. We respond within one calendar month.

12. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that we have collected data from an individual under 18 without verified parental consent, we will delete it and, where relevant, terminate the associated account.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top of this page reflects the current version. Material changes will be communicated by email where required.
