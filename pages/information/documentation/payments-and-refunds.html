Payments & Refunds

This policy explains the payment methods we accept, how deposits and refunds work, and how we handle disputes. It forms part of our Terms of Use.

Effective: 10 October 2026, 13:20 UTC · Version 1.0
Contact: init.nexusbyte@gmail.com

1. Overview

All payments are made in advance unless otherwise agreed in writing. Prices, taxes, and fees are set out at checkout or in your engagement agreement. By paying, you accept this policy.

2. Payment Methods

We accept payment via third-party payment processors, including:

  • Bank transfers
  • Wire transfers
  • PayPal
  • Stripe
  • Square
  • Wise
  • Cryptocurrencies
  • Gift cards

Each processor has its own terms and privacy practices. We do not store full card numbers or cryptocurrency private keys.

3. Deposits, Milestones & Payment Plans

For larger engagements — including certain Technical Services, Development projects, custom builds, and Enquiry Services — we may require:

  • A non-refundable deposit before work begins, to reserve capacity;
  • Milestone payments released as agreed deliverables are completed;
  • A payment plan with scheduled instalments.

Specific terms are agreed in writing before work begins. Deposits become non-refundable once work has commenced, except where required by law or where we fail to deliver the agreed work.

4. Currency, Fees & Taxes

Prices are quoted in GBP (£). Any USD or EUR figures shown are indicative and may vary with exchange rates. You are responsible for any bank, wire, cryptocurrency network, or currency-conversion fees charged by your provider. Where VAT, sales tax, or customs duties apply, they are your responsibility unless expressly stated otherwise.

5. Refund Window — 48 Hours

48-hour refund window. Refund requests must be submitted within 48 hours of delivery for physical goods, or within 48 hours of payment for services (provided the service has not yet begun).

To qualify for a refund:

  • Physical goods — must be unopened, unused, untouched, and unaltered, in original packaging.
  • Services — the service must not have been provided yet, or you must provide a reasonable justification for cancellation that we accept at our discretion.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible, less any non-recoverable processing fees (typically 2–5%).

7. Non-Refundable Items

  • Opened, used, or tampered-with consumables — for hygiene and safety reasons;
  • Age-Restricted Goods once dispatched, where the failure is on the customer's side (failed age verification, refused delivery, or unlawful local possession);
  • Custom or personalised apparel once production has begun;
  • Digital Content once delivered, except where corrupt or misdescribed;
  • Software or subscriptions whose licence or code has been redeemed or activated;
  • Completed Technical Services, Platform Services, or Enquiry Services;
  • Confirmed cryptocurrency payments (see Section 9);
  • Gift-card purchases, except as store credit (see Section 9).

8. Faulty, Damaged, or Incorrect Goods

If you receive a faulty, damaged, or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photographs and your order reference. We will arrange a replacement, repair, or refund in line with your statutory rights (see Section 11). Return shipping for verified faults is at our cost.

9. Crypto & Gift-Card Payments

Cryptocurrency: once a transaction is confirmed on-chain, it is irreversible. We may, at our sole discretion, offer store credit or a fiat-equivalent refund net of network fees and price fluctuation.

Gift cards: refunds for gift-card payments are issued as store credit only, and are subject to the gift card's own terms (including expiry).

10. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first at init.nexusbyte@gmail.com. We aim to resolve disputes within 5 working days.

Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in:

  • Immediate suspension of your account and pending orders;
  • A £25 (indicative: ~$30 / ~€28) administration fee, where permitted by law;
  • Recovery of the disputed amount plus costs where the chargeback is unjustified.

Nothing in this section prevents you from exercising statutory rights with your bank or card issuer.

11. Your Statutory Rights

Our 48-hour refund policy is offered in addition to, and does not replace, your statutory rights. Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 (England & Wales) and equivalent legislation in other jurisdictions, you may have:

  • A 30-day short-term right to reject faulty goods and obtain a full refund;
  • A right to repair or replacement for faults arising within 6 months (and beyond in some cases);
  • A right to a price reduction or final right to reject after one repair or replacement attempt;
  • EU/EEA consumers: a 14-day right of withdrawal for distance purchases, except for sealed goods unsealed after delivery, perishables, and personalised items.

Where these rights exceed our policy, they prevail.

12. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email init.nexusbyte@gmail.com from the address used for the order.
  2. Include your order or invoice reference, the item or service, and the reason for the request.
  3. Attach photographs or evidence where relevant (faults, damage, incorrect items).
  4. We will acknowledge within 5 working days and confirm the outcome.
