### Introduction
Welcome to the Mixterial repository, a collection of code, information, pages and assets that we use universally across all of our web related projects, products and services.

Mixterial is mainly for our use as it's based around our company but parts of it can also be used by the general public. If you do wish to use anything and it contains anything to do with our company, please update it using your own details and information.
